GeoCamera Privacy Policy

TownTable Devs · Effective 4 October 2026

About GeoCamera

TownTable Devs develops GeoCamera: GPS, Photo & GPX, referred to as GeoCamera in this policy. GeoCamera captures photos and videos with location information and lets you record, import, view and export GPX tracks. This policy describes the current Android application.

Camera, microphone and location

Camera access is used to show the preview and capture media. Microphone access is used to record video sound; without it, photos and silent videos remain available. Location access supplies coordinates, GPS accuracy and timestamps for the camera overlay and optional GPX recording. Approximate location is accepted, but is less suitable for route measurement. GeoCamera does not request background-location permission or run a standalone background tracking service.

You control Android permissions in your device settings. You can disable the GPS overlay, GPX recording, address display and map tile separately in GeoCamera settings. Disabling GPX recording does not disable location information in the camera overlay.

Online maps and addresses

When address display is enabled, GeoCamera sends latitude and longitude over HTTPS to BigDataCloud to obtain a locality or address. If that request fails, it may contact OpenStreetMap Nominatim or Photon by komoot with the same coordinates.

When map tiles are enabled or you explore a route map, GeoCamera requests tiles from OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap. Tile coordinates reveal the approximate area being viewed. These providers also receive normal connection information such as your IP address and the app's request identification. Their own policies govern their processing and retention; GeoCamera cannot delete their network logs.

Turn off Show Address and Show Map Tile to stop these camera requests. Opening or moving a GPX map can still request tiles. Local media playback and GPX parsing do not upload your photos, videos or complete track files. Cached map tiles may be reused offline; new tiles and addresses require internet.

Files, sharing and storage

Photos, videos and track files are processed on your device. Location information added to a photo or video becomes part of that media. GPX files can contain precise coordinates, times, GPS accuracy and available altitude measurements.

Android captures may be saved in shared Pictures/GeoCamera or in app-private storage when public saving is unavailable. GPX tracks are normally stored in app-private storage and paired with videos by filename. Imported track copies and map tiles use local cache storage. Unexported private files can be lost when you clear app data or uninstall GeoCamera.

Exporting or sharing is initiated by you. Copies saved to Downloads or other public folders can remain after you delete the original or uninstall the app. A receiving app or person can read location information in the files you share. Their subsequent handling is outside GeoCamera's control.

Accounts, advertising and support

GeoCamera does not require an account and its current build includes no advertising or analytics service. TownTable Devs does not operate a GeoCamera service that receives your recordings or track files.

Report a Bug opens your email app with a draft containing a report template, application identifier and operating-system details. You decide what to add and whether to send it. If you contact support, TownTable Devs receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. Support correspondence is retained as needed to investigate and respond; contact us to request deletion of correspondence held by us.

Your choices and security

Delete original recordings in Gallery and separately delete any exported or shared copies. Android app settings let you revoke permissions and clear local app data or cache. Contact support about information you have sent directly to TownTable Devs. We cannot remove copies held by recipients or independent map and address providers.

Online provider requests use HTTPS. Android app-private storage limits access by other apps, while public exports are subject to Android's shared-storage permissions. Device access, backups you create and files you share affect their security.

Changes and contact

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. Review the policy after app updates for changes in functionality or providers. For privacy questions, contact TownTable Devs at towntable.app@gmail.com.
